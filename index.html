The Course

Bangladesh Scouts, Gopalganj District Rover held its first orientation course on Safe from Harm certification on the night of Friday, 06 November 2026, online through the Zoom app, in cooperation with Bangladesh Scouts Rover Region. The course introduced the Safe from Harm policy of the World Organization of the Scout Movement and guided participants, step by step, through the free Safe from Harm — Essential Learnings (SfH 1) course on the WOSM learning platform, which every member is expected to complete and certify.

Course Contents

  • What Safe from Harm is, and why the Scout Movement adopted it
  • Milestones: the 2002 Thessaloniki resolution, the 2016 position paper, the 2021 amendment of the WOSM Constitution and the Constitution of 2024
  • The six forms of harm: bullying; hate or discriminatory speech; sexual harassment or abuse; online harassment and cyberbullying; neglect of participants; and physical harm
  • Recognising risk, and the safe delivery of programmes and events
  • Receiving complaints and the reporting procedure
  • Opening a scout.org account and completing the SfH 1 certification online
